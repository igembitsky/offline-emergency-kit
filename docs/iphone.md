# The iPhone companion

Put the most important libraries on your iPhone with the free
[Kiwix app](https://apps.apple.com/app/kiwix/id997079563). There is no AI on the phone, but
you can search and read every article with no internet. The phone works when the laptop is
off or out of power.

## Which libraries (about 3 GB)

| In the app, search for | What it is | Size |
|---|---|---|
| WikiMed | Wikipedia medicine, with pictures ("maxi") | 1.7 GB |
| WikEM | Emergency medicine | 360 MB |
| Post Disaster | Post-disaster survival guides | 620 MB |
| Medical Library | Field medicine guides | 70 MB |
| Food for Preppers | Food preparation and storage | 90 MB |
| Water Treatment | Water purification | 20 MB |
| Knots | Knots | 30 MB |

**Optional, if you have space:** "Best of Wikipedia" text only ("nopic", 2.6 GB),
Wikivoyage text only (260 MB), iFixit (3.3 GB).

## Method A: download in the app

1. Connect the iPhone to Wi-Fi and to a charger.
2. Open Kiwix and go to the **Library** tab.
3. On the **Categories** screen, set the language filter to English if you see one.
4. Tap **Other**. In the search field, type `WikEM`. Tap it, then tap **Download**.
5. Go back and do the same for `Post Disaster`, `Medical Library`, `Food for Preppers`,
   `Water Treatment` and `Knots`.
6. Go back to **Categories** and tap **Wikipedia**. Search for `WikiMed`. Tap
   **WikiMed Medical Encyclopedia**, English, the "maxi" version. Tap **Download**.
7. For the options: "Best of Wikipedia" is in **Wikipedia**, Wikivoyage is in the
   **Wikivoyage** category, and iFixit is in **Other**.

## Method B: copy from your Mac

If the kit is on your Mac, the files are already there and verified, in `library/`.

1. Connect the iPhone to the Mac with a cable.
2. In Finder, select the iPhone in the sidebar, then open the **Files** tab.
3. Drag these files from `library/` onto **Kiwix**:
   - `wikipedia_en_medicine_maxi_*.zim` (WikiMed)
   - `wikem_en_all_maxi_*.zim`
   - `zimgit-post-disaster_en_*.zim`
   - `zimgit-medicine_en_*.zim`
   - `zimgit-food-preparation_en_*.zim`
   - `zimgit-water_en_*.zim`
   - `zimgit-knots_en_*.zim`

## Test it

1. Turn on airplane mode.
2. Open each library and read one article.

Update the phone libraries when you update the kit, every 6 months.
